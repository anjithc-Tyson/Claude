# Script 2 — Design Studio image prompts (all new, 13 images)

**Where they appear:** in the studio's preview window (the right side of the bottom panel). So every image is
**square 1:1**, the subject centred and filling about 60–70% of the frame, on a calm background that sits well next to
the studio's cream UI (#F5EFE7).

**Model:** Nano Banana, aspect ratio 1:1.

**Keeping one ring across the video:**
- Generate **P06 first**. It's the "hero design" the whole video builds.
- For P07–P12, **attach P06 (or the image named) as the reference** and keep the words "the exact same ring design".
  That keeps the band shape, setting and stone identical while only the named thing changes.
