# Script 4 — Detail-first hook + match cuts: image prompts

The same hero ring appears in every shot: **a slim 18k yellow-gold band, 2.5 mm wide, with a small oval emerald set flush in the top, and the hand-engraved script inside the band: "A · R 14.02.19".**

Generate the first shot (D01), then use it as the reference image for every later shot so the ring stays the same.

Settings for every shot:
- 9:16 vertical.
- Soft natural window daylight, warm cream and linen tones (matching her wall), true-to-life colour.
- No glow and no dark moody grade.
- Shallow depth of field, photoreal, 85 mm or 100 mm macro look.
- **The subject sits in the centre, at about 35–40 % from the top** (where her face is), so every cut from her to the product lands in the same spot (a match cut).
