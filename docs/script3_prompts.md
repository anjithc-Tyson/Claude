# Script 3 — Journey Tracker image prompts (11 images)

**Where they appear:** as photo thumbnails inside the tracker card, and the last two large (the "Ready" reveal and the
end card). All **square 1:1**, Nano Banana.

**One piece through the whole journey:** this video follows a single custom ring, an **oval emerald in yellow gold with
a fine milgrain edge**. Generate **J10 (the finished ring) first**, then attach it as the reference for J07, J08, J09
and J11, saying "the same ring", so the ring being made is the ring that gets revealed.

Paste this style line at the end of every prompt:
`photorealistic, soft natural window light, warm cream and soft gold tones, shallow depth of field, editorial jewellery photography, no text, no logos, no watermark, square 1:1`
