# Script 4: image prompts v3, "Toi et Moi" two-stone ring

The story: one stone for each person, set facing each other, almost touching. That's "the detail that makes it personal".

Keep the file names D01–D13; I'll swap them straight into the edit, and the timings stay the same.

---

## 1. The hero ring

Paste this block, word for word, wherever a prompt says **[RING]**:

> **[RING]** a fine-jewellery "toi et moi" ring in 18k yellow gold: an open bypass band, hand-sculpted and gently tapering from 2 mm at the back to 1.4 mm where it splits, the two open ends curving past each other in a soft, organic sweep; on one end a pear-cut emerald (7 × 5 mm, about 0.7 ct), vivid deep forest green with natural soft inclusions, held by three fine rounded claws and a delicate V-prong at the tip; on the other end an oval pink sapphire (6 × 4 mm, about 0.6 ct), soft blush-to-rose pink with lively sparkle, held by four fine rounded claws; the two stones sit at a slight angle facing each other, their tips almost touching with a hairline gap of about 1 mm between them; a trail of three tiny round diamonds (1 mm) set flush along each shoulder, getting smaller toward the back; the inside of the band hand-engraved in fine script "A · R 14.02.19"; warm buttery 18k gold, high polish with a soft satin finish on the inside of the band; elegant, airy and modern, sized for a woman's ring size 6

**Why it isn't generic:**
- two different coloured stones, one for each person, instead of one standard stone
- an open, sculpted band instead of a plain round shank
- the 1 mm gap between the two stones becomes the hero detail of the video

**[LOOK]: paste this wherever a prompt says [LOOK]:**
> luxury fine-jewellery campaign photograph, real photograph not CGI, full-frame camera, 100 mm macro lens, f/4, soft natural north-window daylight from the left with a large diffused fill card on the right, true-to-life gemstone colour and brilliance, realistic reflections in polished gold showing the soft window shape, warm cream and oat linen palette, natural skin with fine texture and pores, shallow depth of field, crisp focus on the stones, calm, quiet, expensive, editorial

**[NEGATIVE]: paste into the negative-prompt box if the tool has one:**
> CGI, 3D render, plastic, cartoon, toy, costume jewellery, oversaturated, neon green, orange gold, brassy, chunky ring, thick band, closed round band, single stone, stones touching or fused, oversized stones, two rings, stacked bands, warped or melted metal, extra prongs, floating stones, extra fingers, deformed hands, blurry engraving, misspelled engraving, glitter, sparkle effects, lens flare, glow, dark moody lighting, watermark, text

Rules for every image:
- **Make the master shot first (M00 below), then attach it as the reference image for every other shot.** Write "the exact same ring as the reference image" in each prompt.
- Ask for **9:16, the highest resolution available (2K or 4K)**.
- **Composition:** the main subject sits horizontally centred, about **38 % down from the top** of the frame. That's where her face is in the video, so the cuts match.

## 5. Checklist before uploading

- [ ] The same ring in every image: open band, pear emerald + oval pink sapphire, a tiny gap, shoulder diamonds.
- [ ] Stones never fused together, never a single stone, never two rings (D11).
- [ ] Gold looks buttery yellow, not orange; the emerald is deep green, not neon.
- [ ] Engraving (where visible) reads "A · R 14.02.19".
- [ ] Natural hands with five fingers.
- [ ] 9:16 at the highest resolution; the subject at about 38 % from the top (D13: the box sits low, the top is empty).
- [ ] Files named D01–D13 (+ V01/V10) in a Drive folder.
