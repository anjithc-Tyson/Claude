# Script 4 · "The Detail" — Copperplate Engraving

Takshila, vertical reel (1080×1920, 30 fps, ~52 s). Her own take is the voice and the face of the film; the engraving
plates are the picture around her. Instagram safe zones: no text under the top bar (y < 240), right of x 870 between
y 720 and 1650 (buttons), or below y 1660 (caption row).

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **A book of plates.** Four small plates turned like pages (the person / the moment / the design / the detail), one
   fact each, her between them. Clear, but it is the magazine idea again: static pages.
2. **States of one plate.** One plate of one ring, re-cut three times as the story moves: first the finished ring with
   one spot left blank on the copper, then the three things it was built around engraved as roundels, then the ring cut
   pass by pass, and finally the blank spot burnished and cut: the hidden detail. Colour arrives only at the reveal.
3. **The proof.** Mirrored copper all film, the true paper proof peeled only at the end. Strong single move, but the
   viewer reads mirrored lettering for 40 s.

**Chosen: 2, states of one plate.** It *is* the curiosity gap: the blank spot on the ring is visible from the first
second and only filled at the end, so the plate carries the question the script asks. It moves (cutting, roundels
flying, colour blooming) instead of laying out pages, and it keeps her full screen whenever she is the point.

- Opening image: the finished engraved ring already on the plate, but a small oval on its inner band is **bare
  burnished copper**, and the loupe drifts to it as she says "this is the detail".
- Ending: the same plate, now complete and hand-coloured, with the engraved title and takshila.co cut glyph by glyph.
- Score shape: plucked harpsichord while lines are cut → a held string chord only when the colour arrives → a single
  low pluck on the last letter.

## 2. Logline and arc

A finished ring hides one bare spot; as she tells how a custom piece is made, the plate is cut around a person, a moment
and what they carry, until the spot is engraved: their initials and a birthstone.

Setup (0–6 s): beautiful, but something is missing → Turn (37 s): "made to hold something that really means everything"
→ the detail is cut and coloured → Ending: the finished plate asks the viewer for theirs.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Learn from:** 19th-century natural-history and trade-catalogue plates (centred subject, numbered roundels, roman
  title over italic name, del./sculp. line); high-jewellery maison craft films for the slow, reverent pace at the reveal.
- **Don't take:** any plate, border, title, pose, maison logo, music or shot.

## 6. Cue map

84 bpm, 4/4 (a bar = 2.857 s). Harpsichord (plucked, dry) under the cutting plates; solo cello pizzicato under her
full-screen sections, very low; a held string chord from 39.76 ("everything") to the end of shot 6; harpsichord returns
for the end card and lands a single low pluck on the last letter cut. Music ~half under speech; ducked under her voice.
