# Day 17: the part nobody sees (Watercolor Brush insert)

**What it is:** a painted insert cut into Susanna's talking-head take. It plays from "And here it is" to "…14 February 2024" (about 10 s), and the same painted page is the end card (3.6 s).

**Text stays in the Takshila series style, not the style's default faces:**
- Instrument Serif (title, the engraving)
- Afacad tracked caps (small labels)
- Poppins captions from the main edit (sap green spoken word on light paper)

Everything else follows Watercolor Brush STYLE.md: warm paper, brush strokes with flying white, painting-in as the main motion, a slow push into a detail, no vector lines, no hard cuts.

## Three candidate structures

1. **Specimen + note:** one locked sheet. The ring paints itself (band washes → shadow passes → stone). A slow push into the inside of the band, where the engraving is written in ink like a hand note, with a pen leader to a small label "inside the band".
2. **Finished painting, played backwards:** the finished ring and engraving are shown, washed back to paper by mist, then painted forwards again to the engraving.
3. **Two pages:** page one is the ring from outside, polished and "loud". A page turn shows page two: only the inside of the band, where the engraving writes itself.

## Choice: 1, Specimen + note

It matches her words exactly:
- "here it is" = the ring paints in
- "A + R" = the first letters are written
- "14 February 2024" = the date follows

It also keeps the moment calm and personal, which is the brief ("nothing loud").

## Beats (insert time, s)

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0.0–0.6 | Brush-edge wipe from her shot onto the paper; a soft wet bloom of gold ochre |
| 0.6–3.6 | The ring paints in: outer band wash, inner-band shadow, darker edge stroke, pale blue-grey diamond with a single highlight left as bare paper. Paper-rustle foley per stroke group |
| 3.6–4.0 | The push into the inner band begins (the observer leaning in) |
| ~4.0 ("A + R") | "A + R" written in ink inside the band, left to right, with a paper-coloured halo |
| ~6.3 ("14 February 2024") | "· 14.02.2024" written on, pen leader grows to the small Afacad label INSIDE THE BAND |
| to end | Hold, a slight brush sway, then cut back to her |

**End card:** the finished page, pulled back a little. "What would *yours* say?" in Instrument Serif (terracotta italic "yours"), with the sap-green Poppins button.
